# Getting Started

## Create your workspace

Sign up at app.northwinddesk.example with your work email. The first user becomes the workspace owner. Every new workspace starts a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan, and no credit card is required for the trial.

## Invite your team

Go to Settings > Team and click Invite agents. Each invited agent receives an email link that expires after 7 days. Owners and admins can assign three roles: Owner, Admin and Agent. Agents can answer tickets but cannot change billing or security settings.

## Connect your support email

Under Settings > Channels > Email, add your support address and forward incoming mail to the unique forwarding address Northwind Desk shows you. Verify the address by clicking the link we send. Replies are then sent from your own support address after you add the SPF and DKIM records shown on the same page.

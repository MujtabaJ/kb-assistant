# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

New subscriptions can be refunded in full if you request a refund within 14 days of your first payment. After 14 days, monthly plans are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the paid period.

Annual plans cancelled after the first 14 days receive a prorated refund for the remaining full months, minus a 10% administration fee.

## How to request a refund

Email billing@northwinddesk.example with your workspace name and the invoice number. Refunds are processed within 5 business days and returned to the original payment method. Bank transfer refunds can take up to 10 business days.

## Exceptions

Add-on purchases such as extra AI credits and SMS bundles are non-refundable once used.

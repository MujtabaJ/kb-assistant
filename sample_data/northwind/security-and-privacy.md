# Security and Privacy

## Data storage

Customer data is stored in data centers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU region) or Virginia, USA (US region). You choose the region when the workspace is created, and it cannot be changed later. All data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher.

## Single sign-on and two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication is available on all plans and can be required for all agents by an admin. SAML single sign-on (SSO) with Okta, Azure AD and Google Workspace is available on the Enterprise plan only.

## Data retention and deletion

Tickets are kept until you delete them. When a workspace is closed, all data is permanently deleted after 30 days. Backups are kept for 35 days and then deleted.

## Compliance

Northwind Desk is GDPR compliant and offers a Data Processing Agreement (DPA), which can be signed under Settings > Security > Legal. A SOC 2 Type II report is available to Enterprise customers under NDA.

# Billing and Plans

Northwind Desk is a fictional help desk product used for this demo. All facts here are invented.

## Plans

Northwind Desk has three plans. The Starter plan costs $19 per agent per month and includes email support channels and up to 2 inboxes. The Growth plan costs $49 per agent per month and adds live chat, automations and up to 10 inboxes. The Enterprise plan is custom priced and adds SSO, audit logs and a dedicated success manager.

Annual billing gives a 20% discount on Starter and Growth plans.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Enterprise customers can also pay by bank transfer against an invoice with 30-day payment terms. We do not accept PayPal or cryptocurrency.

## Changing your plan

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period. Only workspace owners can change plans, under Settings > Billing.

## Invoices

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on the first day of each billing period. You can download past invoices as PDF files under Settings > Billing > Invoices. To add a VAT number to invoices, enter it under Settings > Billing > Company details before the next invoice is issued.
